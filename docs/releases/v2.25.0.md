Mérés 2026-10-08-án Magyarországról: HTTP 200, 2,5–6,6 KB kérésenként, 0,2–0,5 s; öt ország: Görögország 29 401, Spanyolország 24 646, Olaszország 22 736, Ciprus 1 235, Málta 110 érkező január 1. óta. A portál az adott naptól összegzi a jelentéseket, ezért a havi szám két összeg különbsége, és a lekérdezés mindig hónap első napjára esik (6 kérés, 6 órás gyorsítótár). Soronként: január 1. óta, az utolsó teljes hónap és a **szokásos hónap** (az előző három mediánja). A sor **mérsékelt**, ha az utolsó hónap legalább 1 000 és legalább kétszerese a szokásosnak (a World Monitor szabálya), különben tájékoztató; az ország saját utolsó jelentésének napja az észlelés ideje (Málta jelentése egy hónapos volt). ## English

According to the [watchlist](../watchlist.json), [World Monitor](https://github.com/koala73/worldmonitor) has 19 new commits since the analysis behind v2.23.0. I went through all of them; one idea fits Crucix, the rest concern World Monitor's own internals (the MCP server, forecast grading, security fixes, China labels, sanctions coverage).

- **Idea taken: UNHCR "arrivals".** A new keyless live source, `UNHCR-Arrivals`: the UNHCR Operational Data Portal's sea and land **arrivals in Europe** (Mediterranean and Atlantic routes) per receiving country. Measured from Hungary on 2026-10-08: HTTP 200, 2.5–6.6 KB per request, 0.2–0.5 s; five countries: Greece 29,401, Spain 24,646, Italy 22,736, Cyprus 1,235, Malta 110 arrivals since 1 January. The portal sums reports from a given date, so a month's figure is the difference of two sums, and the query always falls on the first of a month (6 requests, a 6-hour cache). Per row: since 1 January, the last complete month and the **usual month** (the median of the three before). The row is **moderate** when the last month is at least 1,000 and at least twice the usual (World Monitor's rule), otherwise informational; the country's own newest report date is the observation time (Malta's report was a month old). Licence: CC BY 4.0.
- **New kind: `displacement`**, in the "Security and conflict" lens; its point appears on the conflict layer, at the country centroid (`country-centroid`). The risk model does not change.
- **Left out:** IOM DTM's internal-displacement source (it needs an API key, and Crucix keeps to keyless sources) and UNHCR's refugee-stock situations (overlapping situations, much heavier). The watchlist records the reasons too.
- **Watchlist:** the World Monitor review is recorded (`--mark`, head 1c3ce49), the adopted item is on the "adopted" list.
- **Validation:** 4 new tests ([test/sources-unhcr-arrivals.test.mjs](../../test/sources-unhcr-arrivals.test.mjs): month starts; monthly counts from cumulative sums, the acceleration rule, groups adding up; a hostile, future-dated or old answer and the URL-free error; the 6 request dates, the cache, the normalizer and the lens). Existing pinned lists updated (the kind, the source list, the row caps). Run: the files that use the source registry and all 11 files that use the page, the watchlist and infrastructure tests: 400+ tests, 0 failed; the new test on Node 22 too; `npm run check` passes.
